What you can do

📺
Watch & understand
YouTube gets interactive subtitles with furigana, a dialogue queue, and replay keys — built for studying, not just watching.
🔍
Look up anything
Hover any Japanese word on any website for an instant dictionary popup with readings, meanings, and pronunciation audio.
⚡
Mine in one click
Turn any word or subtitle line into a flashcard — screenshot and audio clip included — without leaving the video.
🧠
Remember for good
Spaced repetition shows each card right before you'd forget it. Import your existing Anki decks and keep going.
📈
See yourself improve
Every video shows a live comprehension score, and your reviews build streaks, stats, and a year-long activity map.
🎨
Make it yours
Pick a colour theme, switch to dark mode, and shape your own card types — the extension matches the app automatically.

2

3
Watch, read, and mine
