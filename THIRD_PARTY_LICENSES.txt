      - Tatoeba example sentences — © Tatoeba contributors, CC BY 2.0 FR.

    Copyright (c) Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG)

    Copyright (c) Tatoeba and its contributors

    Authors: jpdb, Kuuube, Gecko

--- Spaced-repetition algorithms ---------------------------------------------

FSRS / py-fsrs (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler)
    Copyright (c) Open Spaced Repetition
    License: MIT
    https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/py-fsrs
    Immersion Suite's FSRS-6 scheduler is a clean-room 1:1 port of the py-fsrs
    reference implementation.

SM-2 algorithm
    Algorithm by Piotr Woźniak / SuperMemo.
    https://super-memo.com/english/ol/sm2.htm
    The classic spaced-repetition scheduling algorithm. The published algorithm
    is used; no SuperMemo source code is included.
